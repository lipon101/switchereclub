The Best Gaming Laptops Under $2000

2026-09-05 · 8 min read

Gaming laptops used to be a bad deal, but that's changed. You can now get a laptop that plays modern games well for under $2000. The key is knowing what to look for. Here's what matters, and the best gaming laptops under $2000.

What to look for

Before the list, a quick note on what matters. The GPU is the most important part, and you want at least an RTX 4060 or equivalent. You also want at least 16GB of RAM and a decent screen. A 144Hz screen makes a big difference. And you want a laptop that doesn't overheat, because gaming laptops run hot.

ASUS ROG Zephyrus G14

The Zephyrus G14 is one of the best gaming laptops you can buy. It's compact, it's powerful, and it has a great screen. It's got an RTX 4060, 16GB of RAM, and a 144Hz display. It's a great all-rounder, and it's a great value.

Lenovo Legion 5 Pro

The Legion 5 Pro is a bigger, more powerful option. It's got a larger screen, a more powerful GPU, and better cooling. It's a bit heavier, but it's a real workhorse. If you want a laptop that can handle anything, this is a great pick.

ASUS TUF Gaming A15

The TUF Gaming A15 is a budget-friendly option that doesn't skimp on performance. It's got a solid GPU, good RAM, and a decent screen. It's not the prettiest laptop, but it's built to last, and it's a great value.

Pro tip: Don't just look at the specs. Read reviews. A laptop can have great specs and still be bad if it overheats or has a bad keyboard. Reviews will tell you the real story.

Why under $2000 is the sweet spot

Under $2000 is the sweet spot for gaming laptops. You get a machine that plays modern games well, without paying the premium for the top-end models. It's the best balance of price and performance. If you're in the market for a gaming laptop, this is the range to look at.
