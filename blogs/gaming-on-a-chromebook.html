Gaming on a Chromebook: What Works and What Doesn't

2026-09-05 · 7 min read

Chromebooks are cheap, light, and great for school and work. But they're not built for gaming. That said, you can still play games on a Chromebook, and some of them work surprisingly well. Here's what works, what doesn't, and how to get the most out of it.

Browser games are your friend

The best games to play on a Chromebook are browser games. They run in the browser, so they don't need a powerful machine. You can play games like Geometry Dash, Slope, and 2048 right in your browser. Browser games are the sweet spot for Chromebook gaming.

Android games work too

Many Chromebooks can run Android apps, which means you can play Android games. There are a lot of great Android games, and they run well on Chromebooks. Just check if your Chromebook supports Android apps, and you can play a huge library of games.

Cloud gaming is an option

If you want to play bigger games, cloud gaming is an option. Services like GeForce Now let you stream games to your Chromebook, so you can play games that would never run on a Chromebook. It requires a good internet connection, but it works.

Pro tip: Keep your Chromebook updated. ChromeOS updates can improve performance and fix bugs, and they can make your games run better.

Know the limits

It's important to know the limits. A Chromebook isn't going to run the latest AAA games. But if you stick to browser games, Android games, and cloud gaming, you can have a lot of fun. Don't expect too much, and you won't be disappointed.

Gaming on a Chromebook is possible, if you know what to play. Stick to browser games, try Android games, and consider cloud gaming. Do that, and you can have a lot of fun on your Chromebook.
