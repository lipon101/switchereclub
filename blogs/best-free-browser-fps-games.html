The Best Free FPS Games You Can Play in a Browser

2026-09-05 · 7 min read

There's a myth that you need a beefy PC and a big download to play a decent first-person shooter. It's just not true. Browser FPS games have come a long way, and the best ones are fast, competitive, and free. No install, no account drama, no waiting for a 40GB update. You open a tab and you're shooting. Here are the free browser FPS games actually worth playing.

What makes a browser FPS worth it

Before the list, a quick reality check. Browser shooters aren't going to match a AAA title on graphics, and they don't need to. What they need is tight controls, decent matchmaking, and a player base that isn't dead. The games below all have that. They're also light enough to run on a laptop that isn't built for gaming, which is the whole point.

Krunker.io

Krunker is the king of browser FPS for a reason. It's a fast, blocky shooter that runs at a smooth frame rate even on weak hardware, and it has real matchmaking with real players. The movement is quick, the gunplay is snappy, and there are enough game modes to keep you busy. It's the closest you'll get to a proper arena shooter in a browser, and it's completely free.

1v1.LOL

1v1.LOL is a building-and-shooting game that takes the best parts of Fortnite and strips out the grind. You build walls and ramps, you shoot, and you try to outplay your opponent. It's got a real competitive edge, and the building mechanic adds a layer of skill that pure shooters don't have. If you like the idea of Fortnite but don't want the download, this is the one.

Shell Shockers

Shell Shockers is exactly what it sounds like: an egg-based shooter where you play as an egg with a gun. It sounds silly, and it is, but it's also a genuinely solid arena shooter. The controls are simple, the matches are quick, and the egg theme makes it way more fun than it has any right to be. It's a great pick if you want something light and funny.

Bullet Force

Bullet Force is a more traditional FPS, with a proper loadout system, multiple game modes, and a more realistic feel than the blocky shooters. It's a step up in complexity, which is good if you want something that feels like a real shooter. It runs in the browser, and it's free, though it does have some in-game purchases if you want to skip the grind.

Pro tip: If your browser FPS feels laggy, close your other tabs and lower the in-game graphics settings. Most browser shooters have a settings menu, and turning down the quality makes a huge difference on a weak machine.

Why browser FPS games are worth it

Browser FPS games are the perfect middle ground. They're free, they run on almost anything, and they're genuinely fun. You don't need to commit to a download or a powerful PC. You just open a tab and play. If you've been writing off browser shooters, give these a try. You might be surprised how good they are.
