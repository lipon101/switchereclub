How to Type Faster: A Gamer's Guide to Better Typing

2026-08-29 · 7 min read

Typing fast feels like a superpower. You see people flying across the keyboard, and you wonder how they do it. The good news is that typing speed is a skill, and like any skill, it can be trained. Whether you want to type faster for games, for chat, or just to get your work done, here's how to actually improve.

Stop looking at the keyboard

The single biggest thing that holds people back is looking at the keyboard. When you look at the keys, you're not typing, you're hunting. You find a key, you press it, you find the next one. That's slow. The fix is to learn to type without looking. It's hard at first, but it's the only way to get fast.

Start by learning the home row. Your fingers should rest on the A, S, D, F, J, K, L, and semicolon keys. From there, you can reach every other key without moving your hands. It feels awkward at first, but it's the foundation of fast typing.

Use all your fingers

Most people type with two fingers, and that's the biggest limit on their speed. If you use all ten fingers, you can type much faster. It takes practice, but it's worth it. Each finger is responsible for a set of keys, and once you learn the pattern, you'll be typing faster than you thought possible.

Don't try to learn all ten fingers at once. Start with the home row, then add the keys above and below. It's a gradual process, but it works.

Practice with typing games

The best way to get faster is to practice, and the best way to practice is with typing games. There are free typing games that make practice fun. They track your speed and accuracy, and they give you a goal to work toward. It's a lot more fun than typing the same sentence over and over.

Pro tip: Focus on accuracy first, speed second. If you're making mistakes, you're not getting faster, you're getting sloppy. Slow down, type accurately, and the speed will come.

Why typing speed matters for gaming

Typing speed matters more than you think for gaming. In chat, you can communicate faster. In games that need quick commands, you can react faster. And in games that need you to type, you can do it without looking away from the screen. It's a skill that makes you better at everything.

Typing fast is a skill you can learn. Stop looking at the keyboard, use all your fingers, practice with games, and focus on accuracy. Do that, and you'll be typing faster in no time.
