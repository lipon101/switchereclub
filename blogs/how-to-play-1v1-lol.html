How to Play 1v1.LOL: A Complete Beginner's Guide

2026-08-24 · 6 min read

1v1.LOL is the browser game that feels like Fortnite. You build, you shoot, and you try to outplay your opponent. It's fast, it's fun, and it's free. Here's how to get started and actually win.

The basics

The game is a 1v1 battle. You and your opponent are on a small map, and you fight to the death. You can build walls and ramps to protect yourself and get the high ground. The last one standing wins.

How to build

Building is the key to 1v1.LOL. You can build walls, ramps, and floors. The walls protect you, the ramps let you get the high ground, and the floors let you move around. The key is to build fast.

When you're under fire, build a wall. When you want the high ground, build a ramp. The player with the high ground has the advantage, because they can see the whole map.

How to shoot

You have a gun, and you need to aim. The shooting is simple, but the aiming is the hard part. You need to lead your shots and aim for the head. Headshots do more damage.

The high ground

The high ground is everything in 1v1.LOL. If you're above your opponent, you can see them, and they can't see you. Build a ramp, get the high ground, and you'll win most fights.

Practice makes perfect

1v1.LOL is a game of practice. The more you play, the better you get at building and shooting. Don't get discouraged if you lose your first few matches. Everyone does.

Pro tip: The key to winning is to build fast. The player who builds the fastest wins. Practice your building, and you'll win more fights.

The best way to play

1v1.LOL is best played in short sessions. It's fast, it's intense, and it's a real test of your reflexes and building speed. And it's free, so you can play as much as you want.

So, if you want to play a shooter that's actually fun, try 1v1.LOL. You'll be building and shooting in no time.
