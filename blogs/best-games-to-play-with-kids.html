The Best Games to Play With Kids (That Adults Enjoy Too)

2026-09-02 · 7 min read

Playing games with your kids is a great way to spend time together, but it comes with a problem: most kids' games are boring for adults. You end up sitting there, playing something mindless, and counting the minutes. But there are games that kids genuinely love and adults actually enjoy too. Here are the best ones, and they're all free to play in a browser.

What makes a good family game

A good family game is easy enough for a kid to learn, but fun enough for an adult to enjoy. It's not too complicated, it's not too violent, and it's something you can play together. The games below all fit that bill. They're the kind of games that make you both want to play one more round.

Fireboy and Watergirl

Fireboy and Watergirl is a co-op puzzle game where one player controls Fireboy and the other controls Watergirl. You have to work together to get through each level, and it's a game that's genuinely fun for both kids and adults. It teaches teamwork, and it's the kind of game that makes you both think. It's a great family game.

2048

2048 is a simple puzzle game that's easy for kids to learn and surprisingly fun for adults. You slide tiles to combine them, and the goal is to reach 2048. It's a game you can play together, taking turns, and it's a game that's genuinely satisfying. It's also a game that's easy to pick up and put down, which is perfect for family time.

Chess

Chess is a classic family game, and it's a great one to play with kids. It teaches strategy and patience, and it's a game you can play at any level. You can play against your kid, or you can play against the computer and let them watch. It's a game that's genuinely good for kids, and it's a game you can play for the rest of your life.

Snake

Snake is a simple game that's perfect for kids. You control a snake, eat food, and try not to hit the walls. It's easy to learn, it's fun, and it's a game that kids love. It's also a game that adults can enjoy, especially when you're trying to beat your own high score. It's a great game to play together.

Pro tip: Let your kid take the lead. Part of the fun of family gaming is watching them figure things out. Don't solve every puzzle for them, let them have the win.

Why these games work

These games work because they're fun for everyone. They're not too hard for kids, and they're not too boring for adults. They're the kind of games that bring a family together, and they're all free to play in a browser. If you're looking for a game to play with your kids, these are the ones to try.
