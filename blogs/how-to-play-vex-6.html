How to Play Vex 6: The Newest Challenge

2026-09-05 · 7 min read

Vex 6 is the newest game in the Vex series, and it's a fresh challenge. It's got new levels, new mechanics, and new obstacles. It's a real test of skill, but it's also a lot of fun. Here's how to get good at it.

Start with the basics

Even though Vex 6 is new, the basics are the same. It's a precision platformer, and you need to be precise. Take your time, line up your jumps, and be careful. The basics are the foundation of everything else.

Learn the new levels

Vex 6 has new levels, and you need to learn them. Each level has its own layout, its own obstacles, and its own challenges. The key is to learn the levels. Where are the spikes? Where are the gaps? Once you know the layout, you can plan your route.

Use the checkpoints

Vex 6 has checkpoints, and they're your best friend. When you die, you respawn at the last checkpoint. This means you can make progress in chunks. Use the checkpoints to your advantage.

Pro tip: Watch the level before you try to move. If you can see the level, you can plan your route. Knowing what's coming is a huge advantage.

Don't give up

Vex 6 is hard, and you'll die a lot. But that's the game. The key is to not give up. Keep trying, keep learning, and you'll get it. The feeling of beating a hard level is the best part of the game.

Vex 6 is a game of precision and persistence. Start with the basics, learn the levels, use the checkpoints, and don't give up. Do that, and you'll beat the game.
