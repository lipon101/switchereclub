The Best .io Games to Play Online (and They're All Free)

2026-08-20 · 6 min read

.io games are the most addictive games on the internet. They're simple, they're competitive, and they're free. You play against other people in real time, and it's a rush. Here are the best .io games to play.

Agar.io

The game that started it all. You're a cell, and you eat other cells to grow. The bigger you are, the more you can eat. But the bigger you are, the slower you move. It's a game of strategy.

Slither.io

You're a snake, and you eat orbs to grow. The bigger you are, the more you can eat. But you have to avoid the other snakes. It's a game of reflexes.

Diep.io

You're a tank, and you shoot shapes to grow. You upgrade your tank as you grow. It's a game of strategy and reflexes.

Why .io games are great

.io games are great because they're competitive. You're playing against real people, and you want to win. It's a rush. And they're free, and they're instant.

How to get better at .io games

The key to .io games is to be aggressive. You need to eat and grow fast. But you also need to be careful, because the bigger you are, the more you're a target.

Pro tip: In Agar.io, split to eat smaller cells. It's a risky move, but it's the fastest way to grow.

The best way to play

.io games are best played in short sessions. They're fast, they're competitive, and they're dangerously easy to sink an hour into. And they're free.

So, if you're looking for a competitive game, try a .io game. You'll be hooked.
