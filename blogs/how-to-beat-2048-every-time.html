How to Beat 2048 Every Time: The Corner Strategy Explained

2026-08-23 · 5 min read

2048 is a game of luck and strategy. But there's a strategy that wins almost every time. It's called the corner strategy, and it's the key to beating the game. Here's how it works.

The corner strategy

The corner strategy is simple. You keep your biggest tile in a corner, and you only move in two directions. This keeps your tiles organized and gives you room to build.

Here's how to do it. Pick a corner, say the top-left. Keep your biggest tile there. Then, only move left and up. Never move down or right unless you have to.

Why it works

The corner strategy works because it keeps your tiles organized. When you only move in two directions, your tiles stay in a chain. And a chain is easy to merge.

If you move in all four directions, your tiles get scattered, and you run out of room. The corner strategy prevents that.

How to build the chain

You want a chain of tiles, from biggest to smallest, leading up to your corner. So, next to your 2048 tile, you want a 1024, then a 512, and so on. This makes it easy to merge.

What to do when you're stuck

Sometimes you'll get stuck. You'll have no good moves. When that happens, take a breath and look at the grid. There's almost always a move that keeps your corner safe.

Pro tip: If you have to move your big tile out of the corner, do it carefully. Move it back as soon as you can.

Practice

The corner strategy takes practice. The more you play, the better you get at seeing the moves. But once you get it, you'll win almost every time.

So, pick a corner, build a chain, and keep your tiles organized. That's the whole game. And you'll beat it.
